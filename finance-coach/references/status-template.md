# Status file template

Give the person their status file in this layout. Fill in only what you know;
write "unknown" rather than guessing. Numbers on items never change once
assigned, so they can refer to "item 4" across sessions.

---

```markdown
# My Money Status

Last updated: YYYY-MM-DD
Educational notes from an AI coach, not professional financial advice.

## Who this covers
- People: ...
- State: ...
- Military / federal: yes / no (which)

## To do (most important first)

| # | What | Why it matters | Next step | Deadline |
|---|------|----------------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Example: Invest the cash sitting in the Roth IRA | Uninvested cash doesn't grow | Buy one broad index fund | none |

## Key numbers
Each figure says where it came from and when.

| What | Amount | Source and date |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| Monthly take-home | $... | Pay stub, YYYY-MM-DD |
| Emergency fund | $... | Bank statement, YYYY-MM-DD |

## Accounts
| Account | Type | Invested or cash? | Notes |
|---------|------|-------------------|-------|

## Debts
| Debt | Balance | Interest rate | Monthly payment |
|------|---------|---------------|-----------------|

## Still unknown
Questions that need an answer from a document or a phone call.

## Done
| # | What | Date |
|---|------|------|
```

---

## Rules for keeping it

- **Move finished items to Done** with the date. Don't delete them; they show
  progress, and progress keeps people going.
- **Every figure carries a source and date.** A number with no source is a
  guess.
- **Keep the to-do list short.** Three to seven open items. More than that and
  nothing gets done; park the rest under "Still unknown" or a later session.
- **Never store** Social Security numbers, full account numbers, passwords or
  logins in the file.
