# Finance Coach - setup (about 5 minutes)

Finance Coach turns Claude into a patient money coach. It asks about your
situation, gives you a short to-do list, and checks tax limits and deadlines
against official sources instead of guessing.

**It is education, not professional financial advice.** Check big or
irreversible decisions with a licensed professional.

## What you need

- A Claude account at claude.ai (the free plan works)
- The file **finance-coach.zip** (don't unzip it)

## Install it once

1. Go to **claude.ai** and sign in.
2. Open **Settings > Capabilities** and turn on **Code execution and file
   creation**. Skills need this to work.
3. Open **Customize > Skills**.
4. Click **+**, then **+ Create skill**, then **Upload a skill**.
5. Choose **finance-coach.zip**.
6. Make sure Finance Coach is switched **on** in your skills list.

## Use it

Start a new chat and say something like:

> Help me get my finances organized.

Claude will ask a few questions at a time. Answer what you can - "I don't
know" is a fine answer.

## Every time after that

At the end of a session, Claude gives you a **status file**. **Download and
keep it.** Next time, upload it at the start of the chat and say:

> Here's my status file - let's pick up where we left off.

Claude doesn't remember past chats on its own. The status file is its memory.

## Keep yourself safe

- **Cover up** your Social Security number and full account numbers before
  uploading any statement. The last four digits are enough.
- **Never** type a password, PIN or login into the chat.
- If you share money with someone, decide together what to share.
